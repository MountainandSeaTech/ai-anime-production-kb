# 技能魔法特效与提示词模板二十连⑪：传送门/空间魔法

## 核心要点

- **传送门第一死因：只写"swirling portal"**——AI输出的是平面漩涡贴图；必须写四层结构：外环（Frame/Ring）→ 视界（Event Horizon）→ 喉管（Throat）→ 另一端（Far Side），每层给独立描述
- **视界形态决定气质**：球体=稳定/科学感，撕裂分形=危险/混沌，液态表面=神秘/魔法，几何硬边=人工/科技——不写形态就随机
- **空间扭曲三件套**：`gravitational lensing`（引力透镜弯曲星光）+ `spacetime ripples like water`（时空涟漪）+ `chromatic aberration at edges`（边缘色差）——这三词让传送门从"图层"变成"真实存在于空间中"
- **传送门是光源**：wormhole/portal itself as the primary light source，照亮周围环境和人物，必须有`lens flares`和`volumetric shadows`
- **另一端必须可见**：`throat reveals distant alien star system` / `infinite kaleidoscopic tunnel` / `shimmering bridge of pure energy`——看不到另一端的传送门没有叙事功能

## 详细内容

### 一、传送门四层结构

```
LAYER 1 - 外环/框架（Frame）：
  → colossal metallic ring etched with alien runes
  → ancient stone archway carved with glowing sigils
  → hard-edged geometric portal floating in air
LAYER 2 - 视界（Event Horizon）：
  → perfectly spherical singularity
  → pulsating ovoid aperture
  → unstable tearing fractal gateway
  → shimmering liquid-like surface
LAYER 3 - 喉管/漩涡（Throat/Vortex）：
  → exotic matter plasma swirling in violent chaotic vortex
  → graceful slow-moving spiral
  → laminar flow like liquid ribbons
  → crackling electrical storm
LAYER 4 - 另一端（Far Side）：
  → glimpse of distant alien star system with twin suns
  → infinite kaleidoscopic tunnel of light
  → smooth reflective singularity with no visible interior
  → chaotic storm of light-years being compressed
```

### 二、视界形态与语义映射

| 视界形态 | 语义 | 适用场景 |
|---|---|---|
| perfectly spherical singularity | 稳定、科学、可控 | 星舰/科技传送 |
| pulsating ovoid aperture | 有机、生命、呼吸感 | 生物魔法/德鲁伊门 |
| unstable tearing fractal gateway | 危险、混沌、不稳定 | 恶魔入侵/禁忌魔法 |
| shimmering liquid-like surface | 神秘、优雅、镜面感 | 精灵/秘术传送 |
| hard-edged geometric portal | 人工、精确、机械感 | 未来科技/符文工程 |

### 三、空间扭曲三件套（让传送门"真实"）

1. **引力透镜**：`severe gravitational lensing bending and warping light from distant stars into dramatic arcs` / `Einstein ring phenomenon`
2. **时空涟漪**：`visible ripples like water emanating outward` / `shimmering heat-haze effect` / `grid-like pattern visibly bent`
3. **边缘色差**：`chromatic aberration at edges where reality seems to fray` / `prismatic light splitting into base colors`

不写这三项 = 传送门悬浮在背景上；写了 = 传送门扭曲了它所在的空间。

### 四、传送门三形态（科技 vs 魔法 vs 裂缝）

| 形态 | 外环 | 视界 | 内部 | 色彩 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 科技星门 | 金属环+符文 | 液态镜面 | 能量桥 | 蓝/白/银 |
| 魔法漩涡 | 无环/自然形成 | 能量球 | 光隧道 | 紫/金/绿 |
| 空间裂缝 | 撕裂边缘 | 分形撕裂 | 混沌压缩 | 红/黑/橙 |

### 五、常见翻车与修复

| 翻车 | 原因 | 修复 |
|---|---|---|
| 像平面漩涡贴图 | 无分层结构 | 补四层（外环/视界/喉管/另一端） |
| 与背景糊在一起 | 无空间扭曲 | 补引力透镜+时空涟漪+色差 |
| 看不到另一端 | 无喉管描述 | 补`throat reveals distant...` |
| 没有体积感 | 无光照交互 | 补`portal as primary light source` |
| 像卡通圈圈 | 无粒子细节 | 补`filaments of nebula gas`, `cosmic dust` |

## 关键参数 / 模板 / 清单

**模板A：魔法传送门（中景/召唤仪式）**
```
35mm medium shot, slight slow orbit. An ancient stone archway stands
in a ruined temple, carved with glowing celestial runes. Within
the arch, a shimmering liquid-like event horizon pulses with an
otherworldly glow. The throat reveals an infinite kaleidoscopic
tunnel of light, spiraling inward with graceful laminar flow like
liquid ribbons of pure gold and violet energy. Swirling within
the vortex are filaments of glowing nebula gas and streams of
shimmering cosmic dust. The immense energy creates visible
ripples in the air around the portal, like water emanating
outward. Chromatic aberration frays the edges of reality where
the portal meets the stone frame. The portal is the primary light
source, casting long volumetric shadows and intense lens flares
across the temple floor. Cinematic, photorealistic, volumetric
lighting, film grain.
Negative: anime, cartoon, flat circle, no depth, no far side,
uniform swirl, no spatial distortion, clean edges, 2D, oversaturated
```

**模板B：空间裂缝（广角/灾难/入侵）**
```
24mm wide angle, low angle looking up. A massive unstable tearing
fractal gateway tears open in the storm sky above a ruined city.
The event horizon is jagged and irregular, with edges that crackle
with multi-colored lightning. An accretion disk of exotic matter
plasma swirls in a violent chaotic vortex around the rim. The
throat reveals a glimpse of a distant burning world with twin

bending the light from surrounding buildings into dramatic arcs.
The fabric of spacetime itself appears distorted with visible
ripples like water. Chromatic aberration splits light into its
base colors at the fracture edges. Debris and dust are pulled
upward toward the rift. The sky darkens around the portal while
the rift itself burns with fiery orange, deep crimson, and
electric purple. Cinematic, terrifying, photorealistic, film grain.
Negative: anime, cartoon, smooth circle, stable edges, no gravity
effect, no debris pull, flat lighting, clean composition, 2D
```

## 备注

- 海裂映射：深渊之核的巨兽"降临"可用空间裂缝模板B变体——去掉"双太阳"，改为深海生物发光质感，裂缝边缘带有生物组织纹理（living tissue at the tear edges），色彩从红橙转向青紫
- 与375现代异能联动：空间系异能=传送门的"人体版"，提示词写`a localized spatial distortion around the character's hand, gravitational lensing bending the background behind them`
- 与386暗影联动：暗影传送门=液态镜面+深紫核心+触须从边缘伸出，写`the liquid surface of the portal is black-violet ink, and shadow tendrils emerge from its edges`
- 与385圣光联动：神圣传送门=金色环+纯白视界+天使羽毛从喉管飘出，写`golden ring framing a pearl-white singularity, ethereal feathers drifting from the throat`
- 运镜建议：传送门最适合POV穿越（falling into it）和对称居中构图（symmetrical centered）强化仪式感；不稳定裂缝用低角度（low angle）强化压迫感
- 声画对位：传送门开启时，低频嗡鸣（sub-bass drone）→ 中频能量上升（energy swell）→ 高频穿越瞬间（transient spike）→ 另一端环境音（ambience shift）
